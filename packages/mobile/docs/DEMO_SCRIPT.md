# JanVaani Mobile — Complete Hackathon & Evaluator Demo Script

This script guides evaluators through testing all 5 core checking capabilities, Indian language support, and privacy features in JanVaani.

---

## 🎬 Act 1: The "Digital Arrest" Police Impersonation Call

1. Open **JanVaani** on Android, iOS, or Web.
2. In the header, note the **Independent Prototype** badge and the **Emergency 1930** button.
3. On the Home Screen under **"What would you like to check?"**, tap **"Phone Call / Call Script"**.
4. Tap the fictional preset: **"🚨 Digital Arrest Call"**.
5. Notice the input populated with:
   > *"CBI Officer Sharma calling from Crime Branch. You are under Digital Arrest for an intercepted narcotics parcel containing 140g MDMA. Join Skype call immediately."*
6. Notice the safety warning: *"Caller ID can be spoofed; JanVaani cannot verify who is on the line."*
7. Tap **"Analyze Warning Signs"**.
8. **Outcome Observed**:
   - Label: `High-risk indicators` (Red badge).
   - Evidence Clues: Quoted passage *"digital arrest... narcotics... skype"*, explaining that police never detain citizens over video calls.
   - Matching Public Advisory: Cites official notice from the **MHA Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C)** with source link.
   - Recommended Steps: Disconnect immediately; do not transfer money; dial 1930.

## ⚡ Act 2: Electricity Bill Disconnection Phishing SMS

1. Return to the Home Screen and tap **"Suspicious SMS / WhatsApp"**.
2. Tap the preset: **"⚡ Power Cut Tonight"**.
   > *"Dear consumer, your electricity power supply will be disconnected tonight at 9.30 PM from electricity office. Immediately call our discom officer at 9876543210."*
3. Tap **"Analyze Warning Signs"**.
4. **Outcome Observed**:
   - Label: `High-risk indicators`.
   - Evidence Clue: Quoted *"disconnected tonight at 9.30 PM"*, highlighting false urgency and unofficial 10-digit sender numbers.
   - Official Match: Cites **CERT-In Advisory CIAD-2024-0018** on utility phishing campaigns.

---

## 🌐 Act 3: Sovereign Language & 22 Scheduled Languages

1. In the top bar, tap the **Language button** (🌐).
2. The modal displays all **22 Scheduled Languages of India** with native scripts (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gurmukhi, etc.).
3. Select **"हिन्दी (Hindi)"**.
4. **Outcome Observed**:
   - The entire UI dynamically updates to human-reviewed Hindi:
     * Heading: *"आप क्या जांचना चाहते हैं?"*
     * Actions: *"संदिग्ध SMS / व्हाट्सएप संदेश"*, *"फोन कॉल / बातचीत का विवरण"*, etc.
     * Labels: *"उच्च जोखिम संकेतक मिले"*, *"सावधानी से जांचें"*.
5. Open the modal again and select an unreviewed language (e.g. **"தமிழ் (Tamil)"** or **"বাংলা (Bengali)"**).
6. Notice the transparent regulatory badge: *"EN Fallback: Human review pending"* (never silently pretending an English fallback is fully translated).

---

## 💡 Act 4: "Learn Safe Digital Habits" & Skilling Quiz

1. Tap the **"Learn"** tab in the bottom navigation.
2. Select the lesson: **"The Golden UPI Rule: PIN is Only for Sending"**.
3. Review the 3 practical takeaways.
4. Scroll to the interactive quiz:
   > *"You receive a message saying: 'You won ₹25,000 lottery cashback. Click here, scan QR, and enter UPI PIN to receive funds.' What is true?"*
5. Select option 2: *"This is a scam. Receiving money never requires entering a UPI PIN."*
6. Immediate positive reinforcement: **"Correct! A UPI PIN is strictly an authorization for debiting money."**

---

## 🔒 Act 5: 1930 Golden Hour & DPDP Privacy

1. Tap the **"1930 Help"** tab to view the step-by-step 2-hour Golden Hour transaction recovery checklist and direct link to `cybercrime.gov.in`.
2. Tap the **"Privacy"** tab to inspect JanVaani's **Zero Credential Pledge** and DPDP Act 2023 Section 11 Right to Erasure disclosures.
